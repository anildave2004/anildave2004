# MagNav-X

**Navigation and sensing that keep working when GPS doesn't.**

I'm Dr. Anil (Neil) Dave, founder of ND LLC (d/b/a MagNav-X) in Palo Alto, California. We build positioning and sensing systems that use the Earth's magnetic field, quantum magnetometers and sensor fusion. They are designed to keep working where satellite navigation is jammed, spoofed, blocked or simply absent.

## What we're building

| Program | What it is |
|---|---|
| **GM-Nav / Maxwell-Shim** | Quantum magnetic navigation for GNSS-denied environments: magnetometer, IMU and GNSS fusion matched against magnetic-anomaly maps |
| **MagNav-X Sentry** | Passive magnetic sensing for counter-UAS detection |
| **Sentinel-10** | Guardrails and intrusion defense for multi-agent AI systems |
| **MagNav-X Med** | Magnetic localization of devices inside the body |
| **Cuffless BP wearable** | Continuous blood-pressure monitoring without a cuff |

## Where it applies

- 🛰️ **Defense and aerospace:** alternative PNT when GPS is denied or spoofed
- 🏙️ **Logistics and delivery:** dependable positioning in urban canyons, inside buildings and on remote routes
- 🤖 **Robotics and autonomy:** indoor and outdoor localization for robots, drones and vehicles
- 🩺 **Medical devices:** tracking and localization in the body, physiological monitoring
- 🔐 **AI security:** keeping agentic AI systems within safe bounds

## Technology

NV-diamond and chip-scale magnetometry · Extended Kalman filtering · Physics-informed neural networks for bias correction · Magnetic-map matching · Signed, spoof-resistant location attestation · US-sourced silicon roadmap
